# Self-hosted fonts

All fonts here are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt).

- `manrope-latin-variable.woff2`: Manrope (latin subset, weights 400 to 800), from Google Fonts.
- `noto-sans-arabic-arabic-variable.woff2`: Noto Sans Arabic (arabic subset, weights 400 to 700), from Google Fonts.
- `noto-sans-sc-*.woff2`: Noto Sans SC.

They are bundled so production builds never download fonts from Google.
